# Campaign Contract

Horizontal campaign:

```text
campaigns/NN-slug/
  article.md
  evidence.md
  review.md
  campaign.json
  youtube.md
  patreon.md
  voiceover.md
  voiceover.ssml
  audio/.gitkeep
```

```text

  article.md
  evidence.md
  review.md
  campaign.json
  youtube.md
  patreon.md
```

## Messaging Guardrails

Prefer:

- portable trust
- sovereign systems
- recognition and revocation
- verifiable trust state
- protocol interoperability
- signed evidence
- independent operators

Avoid framing Genesis Mesh as only:

- an AI agent framework
- a marketplace
- a central identity provider
- a cloud vendor product
- a blockchain/reputation-score system

## Required Review Packet and Publication Gate

Every new campaign or material revision must follow [editorial-evidence-standard.md](editorial-evidence-standard.md) and [design-system.md](design-system.md). Add `evidence.md` (claim ledger, pinned sources, versions, surface coverage, verification results and reader links) and `review.md` (exact reviewed revisions/hashes, review decisions, blockers and approval references). Private or unreleased details stay in an ignored local review packet; do not expose them in the public campaign folder.

The baseline JSON above remains compatible with current scripts. `source_docs` should identify precise evidence, including immutable public links where available, not only vision/strategy files. `status` is a label, not proof of review or permission. Keep it `draft` until applicable evidence, editorial, technical and visual gates pass; never infer approval from a status field. Existing tooling does not automatically enforce this gate.

Do not proceed to voiceover until the exact article is approved. Do not publish, schedule or push campaign drafts to the public repository until explicit publication approval covers the final content/assets and channels. Record verified public URLs only after the action succeeds; do not fabricate release or publication metadata.

## Series Cover Exception

Series campaigns can also contain `thumbnail.png`, generated by `videos/scripts/generate_series_thumbnails.py` for article/Patreon use. With production metadata present, its configured upload copy remains under `videos/thumbnails/`; both copies must match. Rendered video still belongs only under `videos/renders/`. See the design system for exact layout and asset checks.
