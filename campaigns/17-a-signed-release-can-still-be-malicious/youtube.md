LiteLLM's October 2026 incident shows why a real publisher and a valid signature are not enough to establish that a release is safe. The authority behind the pipeline matters too.

This campaign examines scoped credentials, policy overrides, revocation and separately retained evidence. Genesis Mesh is portable trust for sovereign systems, not a guarantee against compromise. Its boundaries help only where they are enforced, and a signed record does not prove that the signer or the artifact was safe.

Inspect one release boundary: who can change its policy, override its approvals, and alter its evidence?
