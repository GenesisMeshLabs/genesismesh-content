Hiding the rest of a capability list does not necessarily hide who issued it.

Genesis Mesh's beta selective-disclosure functions let a verifier check one capability against a signed Merkle commitment. The actual artifacts still expose agreement, issuer and prover identifiers, plus other metadata.

The article examines those fields and the limits of unsalted capability hashes. It separates selective transmission from issuer hiding, anonymity and fresh authorization.

IRAC supplies the research question; it is not a claimed Genesis Mesh feature.
