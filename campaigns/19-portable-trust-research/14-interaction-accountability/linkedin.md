"Done" is not an execution record.

For a workflow crossing organizational boundaries, an audit needs to distinguish the signed authorization decision from the executor's report of what happened.

Genesis Mesh's beta execution workflow links signed records through sequence numbers and prior digests. That helps detect reordered or altered records. It does not prove a truthful outcome or a complete history: a valid prefix can still verify.

The article explains the flow, the three-record example and the checks an integration must supply.
