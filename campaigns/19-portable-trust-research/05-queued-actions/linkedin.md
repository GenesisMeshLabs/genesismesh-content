The action ran. Uploading its evidence failed. Retrying the action would be the wrong recovery.

The documented Genesis Mesh governed-action helper separates the action callback from its evidence outbox. A verified deny never calls the callback. After execution, the outbox retries the report, not the external effect.

That is useful machinery, but it is not a universal queue fence or provider cancellation system. The new draft traces exactly where a revocation can still stop work and where reconciliation is needed.
