An agent can claim to be the finance assistant. A runtime needs different evidence.

The Genesis Mesh reference peer validator checks a signed certificate, network, validity, known revocation state and binding to the connection key. Roles come from the certificate.

That establishes a bounded identity result. The resource owner still decides whether this identity may access this dataset, and stolen keys or unseen revocations remain operational limits.

The article also explains why a historically valid signature can differ from a credential acceptable for a new session.
