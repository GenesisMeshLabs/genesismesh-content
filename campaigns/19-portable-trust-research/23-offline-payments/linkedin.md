Offline authorization and offline payment are different guarantees.

A pre-issued grant can bound a device's permitted actions and support later examination of signed use records. It does not reserve funds, settle a payment or prevent double-spending across disconnected wallets.

This article uses Genesis Mesh's documented grant flow to separate those responsibilities, with PayOff as the payment-research context.
